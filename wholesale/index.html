闸门是关键:选一个欠太多的客户,信用检查当场把整条流挡住 —— 派车、签收、结算全部停。钱没清,货不动。这就是一套系统和六套系统的差别。 The gate is the point: pick a customer who owes too much and the credit check stops the whole flow — dispatch, sign-off, settlement all halt. Money not cleared, goods don't move. That's the difference between one system and six.
